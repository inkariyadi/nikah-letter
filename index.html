A Letter for You

13 October 2026

My dearest,

I have been trying to find the right way to say this, and a letter felt right. Words written slowly, with a song playing softly behind them, seemed better than a quick message.

Time moves while we are busy living. One day is an ordinary morning, and the next you are standing somewhere you once only imagined. I find myself wanting to hold on to the small things, like how you laugh before the joke is finished, how you notice when someone needs a hand, and the sound of you coming through the door.

The photographs above are a few of the moments I carry with me. I did not know how much each of those days would matter until they had passed. I am grateful for every one of them, the ordinary ones most of all.

I am not good at saying this out loud, so I will write it here. I am proud of you, for who you are and for how you treat the people around you. Wherever the next chapter takes you, there is always a place for you, and someone who is glad you exist.

Take your time, stop for the flowers along the way, and know that I am cheering for you in everything.

With all my love,

Always yours
